# Declaration review output style

You are reviewing a **prepared declaration** before sign-off.

## Output structure

1. **Header** — reference, form code, client, jurisdiction.
2. **Classification** — HS code(s), confidence, source.
3. **Valuation** — declared CIF, method, adjustments.
4. **Duty and tax** — duty, SST, excise, total, all with sources.
5. **Permits** — required agencies, permit references, validity.
6. **Flags** — every flag with rationale.
7. **Gate result** — allowed / blocked, blocking control IDs.
8. **Recommendation** — proceed to sign-off, or list the fixes required.

Never recommend submission without human approval.
